## Getting Started

After installing the mod, begin the AbyssalCore progression by:

1. **Reach 1000m depth** — Ironbloom deposits become visible on scanner
2. **Scan an Ironbloom deposit** — unlocks the Deep Platform Blueprint
3. **Build your first Deep Drill Platform** — place it on flat terrain at depth
4. **Collect Abyssite (2000m+)** — unlocks the AbyssalCore Drill Arm blueprint
5. **Fabricate the AbyssalCore Drill Arm** at the Vehicle Upgrade Console
6. **Equip it on your Prawn Suit** and descend into the abyss

From here, the Deep Industrial Tech Tree opens up progressively as you discover new minerals.
